The intelligence behind the experience

Signals become context.
You bring the meaning.

Energy is not something a single sensor can measure. Enlumora’s intelligence system connects available information to help you reflect and choose—not to tell you how you must feel.

  1. 01 · NOTICE

    Read the moment

    Available health readings, their freshness, and the feelings or space context you choose to share.

  2. 02 · UNDERSTAND

    Put it in context

    Body and mood estimates offer a starting point. Your check-in adds your own perspective.

  3. 03 · RESPOND

    Choose one next step

    Talk with Coach, try Qi practice, rest, or make a small adjustment to your surroundings.

  4. 04 · REFLECT

    Notice what helps

    Look back at your patterns and tell Coach what worked. Personalization starts with your feedback.

Body signals do not directly measure emotions. Estimates can differ from how you feel. Notifications require permission, and delivery depends on available data and system background scheduling—not continuous monitoring.
