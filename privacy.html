Privacy

Your data should feel like yours.

Last updated October 10, 2026

Enlumora is a personal wellness companion for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Accounts and sign-in

Enlumora is provided by Jing ArtTech Studio. Sign-in uses Sign in with Apple or a one-time email code. We store an account identifier, your email address (which may be an Apple private relay address), and a display name if you provide one through Apple. These are used to authenticate you, operate your account, and associate any backups you choose to enable. We do not receive your Apple password or ask for an email inbox password. Our email delivery provider, Resend, processes the recipient address and verification email to deliver your sign-in code.

Data used by the app

With your permission, Enlumora may use health and activity information, device connection status, mood and body check-ins, Coach messages, practice history, approximate environmental context, and room photos that you choose to provide. These signals help create personal energy summaries, Coach responses, space readings, and practice suggestions.

Health data

Apple Health access is permission-based. Enlumora may read heart-rate variability, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, active energy, walking or running distance, flights climbed, and respiratory rate. The app uses these signals to create on-device wellbeing estimates and trends. Raw HealthKit samples are not sent to Enlumora’s AI Coach or used for advertising, marketing, data mining, or medical diagnosis. You can change Health permissions at any time in the Health app.

Optional account backup and restoration

Signing in does not automatically upload your raw Apple Health data or turn on account backup. If you agree to enable backup in Profile, body-energy and mood estimates, check-ins, practice records, reflections, and completed practice days are saved to Enlumora’s server under your account for history and restoration. These derived wellness records can still be sensitive health-related information, even though they are not raw HealthKit samples.

You can separately choose whether the backup includes Coach conversations and memory, personal settings, saved space analyses and compressed copies of selected photos, or the locations and times of area analyses. Photo-library access and continuous location history are not part of this backup. Photos and location backup are off by default. Backup consent is separate from permission to share information with AI providers.

Camera and space scans

The camera or photo picker is used only when you initiate a space scan. Before the first AI scan, Enlumora asks for permission to send the selected room photo to its AI service provider for analysis. Avoid images containing people or sensitive details. A local copy is saved only when you choose to save the space.

AI features

Coach messages and room-photo analysis are processed through Enlumora’s service and OpenAI. Coach personalization is optional: before derived energy, mood, space, check-in, or practice labels are included, the app asks for your consent. Raw HealthKit samples are not included. Information sent for AI features is limited to what is needed to provide the requested response and is not used by Enlumora for advertising.

Device and service data

Enlumora uses installation/device identifiers and service request information to operate the service, prevent abuse, and apply rate limits. Location features use your permission to obtain coordinates for nearby context, weather, and walking routes. These requests may be processed by Apple mapping services, our service, and weather providers such as Open-Meteo. If you separately enable location backup, saved analysis locations and timestamps are linked to your account. Enlumora does not continuously track your location in the background or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Voice and notifications

If you use voice input, microphone and speech-recognition permissions allow Apple’s speech services to transcribe what you say; processing may occur on your device or Apple’s servers. The resulting message is sent to the Coach when you submit it. Notification permission controls reminders and updates, not Health data access. You can turn notifications off in system Settings without disabling energy calculation.

Storage and deletion

Records are stored on your device and, when you enable backup, on our account service hosted using Amazon Web Services. Pausing backup stops future synchronization; it does not delete a backup already stored. Turning off a backup category removes that category from the backup on the next successful sync, while local copies remain. Profile provides controls to delete the account backup.

You can delete your account in Profile > Your account > Delete account; a fresh sign-in may be required. This removes the account and its associated records and saved images from our active service storage, and revokes the app’s Apple authorization for an Apple-linked account. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your server account or backups. Limited operational records may be retained where necessary for security or legal obligations. Service providers may retain data under their own applicable policies; account deletion does not undo requests already processed by them.

We retain account records while your account exists and backups until you delete them or the account. We do not sell wellness data or use it for advertising. For access, correction, or deletion questions, contact us through the Support page; please do not include health information in your first message.

Your choices

You can decline optional permissions, keep account backup off, choose backup categories, use Coach without personalized wellness labels, disconnect supported devices, or delete records and your account. Without readable Health signals, personalized body-energy estimates may be unavailable; we do not treat missing measurements as proof of low energy. We request permission before the corresponding optional features use your data.

Important note

Enlumora provides wellness reflection and educational Qi Flow guidance. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency support.
